Tian Bai
University of Bergen

Tian Bai | 白 天

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Bergen (UiB), working in the Bergen Algorithms Research Group with Prof. Fedor Fomin. Previously, I held a postdoctoral position at the University of Hong Kong (HKU) in the ALGO Lab with Prof. Zhiyi Huang. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC) as a member of the Algorithms and Logic Group, under the supervision of Prof. Mingyu Xiao.
My research is centered on the design and analysis of algorithms, particularly exact and parameterized algorithms for graph problems. I also have a strong interest in algorithmic game theory.
